What's in it

  • All your contacts, meetings and labels (the database)
  • All the original pictures, exactly as you uploaded them
  • A short README with the date and how to restore it

The small preview copies of the pictures are left out: the app makes them again by itself.

Where to keep it

The zip lands in your Downloads folder, on the same computer. If that computer breaks or is lost, it would be lost with it. So copy it somewhere else:

  • A cloud drive (Google Drive, OneDrive)
  • A USB stick or an external disk

Don't upload it to GitHub: it contains private data about the people you know.
